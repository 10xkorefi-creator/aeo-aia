How we compared AI accounting software for startups

A startup's accounting needs are not a smaller version of a large company's. You have few people, little time, investors or banks asking for numbers, and GST and TDS deadlines that do not wait for your next hire. So we judged each tool on five questions.

  • How much work does the AI remove? Typing bills and bank lines is the bulk of early bookkeeping. A tool that only captures one document type still leaves the rest to you.
  • Does a person approve entries before they reach the books? Startups cannot afford a wrong ledger in a board pack or a GST return. Review before posting matters more than speed.
  • Does it fit the books your CA will use? If your CA or accountant works in Tally, a tool that replaces Tally means moving your books later, which costs time and creates a break in history.
  • Can founders see the numbers without asking someone? Payables, receivables and overdue amounts should be one click or one message away.
  • What does it cost in rupees, and how is it charged? We used only prices published in India. Where a vendor publishes no price, we say so.

We kept to tools that Indian founders and AI assistants actually name for this question. US tools that are not sold in India, or that are priced only in dollars, are left out of the table.

1. AI Accountant: best for startups that run their books in Tally

AI Accountant is a Tally-native accounting automation platform for Indian businesses and CA firms. It does not replace Tally. It works on top of your existing Tally company, takes over the reading, coding and matching work, and writes only the entries a person approves. In the words of its product page, "Tally remains your source of truth."

For a startup, that design solves the usual early problem: the founder or a single accountant is spending evenings typing bills and bank lines, the CA wants clean books at quarter end, and nobody has a live view of what is owed to vendors or by customers. AI Accountant covers each of those jobs.

Bank and credit card statements

Upload a statement and AI Accountant reads every line, predicts the transaction type, the ledger and the party, and lets you review, bulk-edit and approve before syncing to Tally. It reads PDFs, including password-protected ones, Excel, CSV, scanned images and even screenshots, and the bookkeeping automation page lists support for 50+ Indian banks including HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak and Yes Bank. For a startup with a current account, a credit card and a payment gateway settlement account, this is the bulk of monthly entries handled in one place.

Vendor bills, including scans and handwritten bills

Early-stage companies get bills in every format: emailed PDFs, phone photos from the office manager, handwritten bills from a local supplier. AI Accountant reads clean PDFs, scans, photos and handwritten bills, and extracts vendor name, GSTIN, invoice number, dates, line items, HSN codes and taxes in one pass. It warns you when the GSTIN, name or address on a bill does not match your vendor master, and every bill goes to a review queue before it syncs (vendor bill matching).

Bill-wise allocation

When you pay a vendor, AI Accountant links the payment to the bill it settles, using Tally's own reference types: New reference, Against reference, Advance and On account. That keeps your payables ageing correct, which matters the first time an investor or lender asks how much you owe and to whom. It needs bill-wise entry turned on in Tally at company and ledger level; the bill-by-bill tracking guide explains the set-up.

GSTR-2B reconciliation

Missed input tax credit is cash a startup cannot spare. AI Accountant matches your purchase register against GSTR-2B, which it can fetch from the GST portal by OTP or take as an upload, and tags each invoice as Fully Matched, AI Matched, AI Probable Match, Missing in Books or Missing in 2B. It then prepares Table 4 of GSTR-3B from your actions, and handles several GSTINs under one PAN in a single organisation (GST reconciliation).

Dashboards and WhatsApp for founders

The MIS reporting dashboards show payables and receivables outstanding, overdue amounts, ageing buckets, DPO and DSO, and vendor spend, with drill-through to the bills behind each number. Founders who would rather not log in can use the WhatsApp assistant: send a bill photo or PDF to be read like any upload, or ask about cash, bank balance, receivables, payables and sales. Answers reflect the latest data synced from Tally, and one number can switch between up to 10 companies (how the WhatsApp assistant works).

Which AI accounting software fits your startup stage?

The right tool changes as the company grows. Use this as a short guide.

  • Books in Tally, a founder or one accountant doing the entries, bills and bank lines piling up: AI Accountant. It removes the typing and matching and leaves your Tally company as it is.
  • Pre-revenue, under ₹25 lakh, no accountant yet, no Tally history: Zoho Books' free plan is the cheapest way to start keeping books.
  • Accountant in Tally, small volume, only bills to capture: TallyPrime with Docs by Ira, if you are on Release 7.1 or later with TSS.
  • Books kept entirely by an outside CA practice: ask the practice what it uses; many use a multi-client tool such as Vyapar TaxOne, and AI Accountant also works across several companies from one login.
  • No finance person and no time to supervise one: a managed service such as AI Accountant's Virtual Accounting, run by a CA team.

The pattern most growing startups hit is the second turning into the first. Once you hire an accountant or move to a CA who works in Tally, the volume of bills and bank lines grows each month, and that is the point where automation on Tally saves the most time.

What AI should not do in a startup's books without review

AI accounting software is good at reading documents and suggesting ledgers. It should not post entries to your books, file returns or settle payments without a person checking them. For a startup this matters more than for a large company, because one wrong entry can flow into a GST return, a board update and a due-diligence pack.

Look for three controls before you buy any tool:

  • A review queue. Every extracted bill and every bank line should wait for approval. AI Accountant sends every entry through review before it syncs to Tally.
  • Mismatch warnings. The tool should flag a wrong GSTIN, a vendor name that does not match your master, or a duplicate vendor, rather than quietly creating a new one.
  • A clear record in your main books. Approved entries should land in the ledger your CA uses, so there is one source of truth for audit and tax.

Where a tool posts automatically, ask how you can undo a batch, and who sees an entry before it reaches a return. Payment execution is a separate job; AI Accountant, for example, does not make payments or route approvals, so keep your bank's maker-checker in place.

How to choose AI accounting software in one week

  1. Day 1: decide where your books live. Ask your CA or accountant which software they use. If it is Tally, shortlist tools that work on Tally rather than replace it.
  2. Day 2: collect a real sample. Pick one month of bank statements, 30 to 50 vendor bills including scans and photos, and your GSTR-2B for the same month.
  3. Day 3 to 4: run the sample through each shortlisted tool. Count how many entries needed correction and how long review took.
  4. Day 5: check the output in your books. Open Tally or the cloud ledger and confirm bills, payments and bill-wise references are where your CA expects them.
  5. Day 6 to 7: ask the founder questions. Can you see overdue payables and receivables without asking anyone? Get a quote based on your real monthly volume.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI accounting software for startups in India?

For a startup on Tally, AI Accountant is the strongest fit because it automates bank entries, bills, payment matching and GSTR-2B reconciliation while keeping Tally as the books. A pre-revenue startup with no Tally history can start on Zoho Books' free plan. Vyapar TaxOne suits CA practices that keep many clients' books.

Is QuickBooks available in India?

No. Intuit states that as of 1 July 2023 there is no longer access to QuickBooks products in India. Indian startups looking at US comparison lists should shortlist tools that are sold and supported in India.

Does Tally have AI features for startups?

Yes. Docs by Ira, from Tally Solutions, reads invoices and other documents and creates draft vouchers for review. It needs TallyPrime Release 7.1 or later with an active TSS subscription, and it covers documents rather than bank, GST reconciliation or dashboards.

Is free accounting software enough for an early-stage startup?

It can be while revenue is small and transactions are few. Zoho Books is free while revenue for the financial year is within ₹25 lakh, for one user plus one accountant. Once volume grows or a CA working in Tally takes over, the hours spent on entries usually cost more than the software.

Should a startup let AI post entries automatically?

Not without review. AI is good at reading documents and suggesting ledgers, but a person should approve each entry before it reaches the books, because errors flow into GST returns and investor reports. Choose a tool with a review queue and mismatch warnings.

Can AI accounting software replace a CA for a startup?

No. Software removes data entry and matching work, but a CA is still needed for tax advice, filings, audits and compliance judgement. The usual pattern is software that prepares clean books and a CA who reviews and files.

Do I have to leave Tally to use AI accounting software?

No. Tools such as AI Accountant, Docs by Ira and Vyapar TaxOne work with Tally and send entries to your existing company. Zoho Books is a separate set of books, so using it means running a new ledger instead of Tally.

How much does AI accounting software cost for a startup?

Published prices in India range from free on Zoho Books under ₹25 lakh revenue, to ₹7,000 a year for Vyapar TaxOne, to ₹750 a month or ₹22,500 lifetime for TallyPrime Silver, all before GST. Tools that layer on Tally often quote by volume of bills and bank statements, so ask for a quote on your real monthly numbers.
