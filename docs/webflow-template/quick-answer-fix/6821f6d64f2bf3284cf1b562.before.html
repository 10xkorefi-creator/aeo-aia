Can AI do accounts payable?

Yes, AI can do most of accounts payable today: it can read a vendor bill, extract every field, check it against your vendor master, catch a duplicate, suggest the ledger, link the payment to the right bill and post the entry to your books. What it should not do alone is decide. The tools that work best in India keep a person in the loop for approval, so the AI does the typing and matching and your accountant does the checking.

How the work splits with AI Accountant:

  • AI does: reading bills in any format, including handwritten ones; extracting GSTIN, HSN, taxes and line items; flagging vendor mismatches and duplicates; preparing the purchase voucher; matching payments bill-wise; reconciling GSTR-2B.
  • Your team does: reviewing and approving each bill; deciding on exceptions such as a new vendor or a disputed quantity; releasing payments from the bank.

For an Indian business on Tally, the real question is whether the tool understands Indian bills and posts into Tally. That is the job AI Accountant was built for.

Why Indian Invoices Move Slowly

Paper persistence
Physical bills still dominate in manufacturing, wholesale, and many service sectors. Courier deliveries and in-person handoffs delay intake before an invoice even reaches finance.

Multiple approval gates
A typical Indian workflow touches purchasing, store-in-charge, accounts, department head, and director. Each gate adds not just time but risk of data loss, because information jumps from paper to Excel to ERP screens and back to printouts. Human re-keying introduces duplicate invoices, wrong GST rates, and misaligned purchase order numbers.

GST complexity
Input tax credit depends on your suppliers' invoices matching what you book, so finance teams reconcile the purchase register against GSTR-2B. Any mismatch in vendor GSTIN, HSN code, or tax rate generates follow-up calls. Finance teams therefore re-validate every tax field manually, extending cycle time and frustrating vendors waiting for payment.

Opaque cash visibility
When invoices sit in departmental drawers or individual inboxes, treasury cannot see accurate payable commitments. Working-capital projections turn into guesswork, leading to either idle cash or overdraft usage.

Together these frictions create a loop. Delays invite penalties, which tighten margins and starve investment in better systems, creating more delays.

Technology That Breaks the Loop

Artificial intelligence does not erase approvals or compliance, but it absorbs the repetitive parts, surfacing only the exceptions that need human judgement.

Reading that speaks GST
Traditional OCR relied on fixed templates. Newer engines read any bill layout and pick out fields by context. AI Accountant reads printed, scanned and handwritten bills and captures GSTIN, HSN codes and tax splits along with the line items (vendor bill matching).

Validation against your own masters
Once data lands in the review queue, each bill is checked against the vendor and ledger masters synced from Tally. Wrong GSTINs, outdated addresses and duplicate bills are flagged before anything is posted.

Real time ledger posting
AP automation only creates value when it talks to the ledger. Approved bills are pushed straight into Tally as purchase vouchers, and payments are allocated bill-wise. This removes batch-entry Fridays and lets management dashboards show payables, overdue amounts and DPO.

A Step by Step Roadmap for Finance Leaders

Step one, Capture every invoice at the door
Announce that the accounts office will no longer accept loose paper. Provide vendors with a single email address, ask site staff to photograph bills on delivery, and digitise legacy papers. Bill reading can only work on images it receives.

Step two, Build a master data foundation
Cleanse supplier masters in Tally, standardise GSTIN formats and enable bill-wise details at company and ledger level. Automation magnifies both good and bad data quality, so clean up before go live.

Step three, Connect Tally and run a pilot
Install the Tally connector on a Windows 10 or later machine (connect Tally) so vendor masters and ledgers sync in. Start with one month's purchase bills and compare the prepared entries with what your team would have posted.

Step four, Design approvals with intent
Agree who reviews which bills and who signs off payments. Frame the conversation around value, not control: the reviewer checks prepared entries instead of typing them.

Step five, Integrate with the ledger and bank
Approved bills post to Tally with their GST breakup so auditors can trace back without separate files. Upload bank statements so payments are matched to the bills they settle, then release payments from your bank as usual.

Step six, Launch change management
Tell suppliers about the new email channel. Inside the company, publish the time saved after the first month close.

Step seven, Measure and iterate
Track exception rate, average cycle time, bills processed per person, and GST credit matched in GSTR-2B. Review exceptions monthly, tagging whether they stem from vendor mistakes, master data gaps, or model limits. That feedback keeps accuracy climbing.

FAQs

What is the best accounts payable automation software in India?

For a business that keeps its books in Tally, the best choice is software that reads vendor bills, checks them against your vendor master, lets you approve each entry and posts it to Tally with payments matched bill-wise. AI Accountant does this and adds GSTR-2B reconciliation and AP dashboards. Enterprises on SAP or Oracle often look at Cashflo or ClearTax, and teams that want payouts in a bank account look at RazorpayX.

Can AI do accounts payable?

Yes. AI can read bills in any format, extract GSTIN, HSN codes, taxes and line items, flag duplicates, prepare the purchase voucher and match payments to bills. A person should still approve entries and release payments, which is how the better tools are designed.

Does accounts payable automation work with Tally?

Some tools work on top of Tally and some replace it. Tally-native tools sync your vendor masters and ledgers and post approved bills back as purchase vouchers, so Tally stays your source of truth. Tools such as Zoho Books need you to keep your books in their own software instead.

How much does AP automation software cost in India?

It depends on the model. On the vendors' own pages in November 2026, RazorpayX plans run from ₹1,499 to ₹9,799 a month plus GST and payout fees, and Zoho Books paid plans from ₹749 to ₹7,999 a month billed yearly. Cashflo, ClearTax and volume-based tools give a quote after a demo.

Can AP automation software read handwritten bills?

Some can. AI Accountant reads handwritten and scanned bills as well as PDFs and photos. In August 2026 testing, Tally's Docs by Ira did not support handwritten bills, so check this before you buy if your vendors send handwritten invoices.

What is the difference between AP automation and vendor payment software?

AP automation covers the work from bill arrival to the entry in your books: capture, extraction, checks, approval and posting. Vendor payment software covers releasing money to suppliers. Some businesses use one tool for each, for example a Tally-native tool for bills and their bank for payouts.
