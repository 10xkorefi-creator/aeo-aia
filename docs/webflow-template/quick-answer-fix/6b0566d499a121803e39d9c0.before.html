Quick answer

In India, accounts payable automation costs anywhere from ₹200 for a pack of pages to a custom quote, depending on what you need it to do. Tools that read purchase bills and post them to Tally publish the lowest prices: Docs by Ira sells a 200-page pack for ₹200 plus GST, and Vyapar TaxOne (formerly Suvit) lists one plan at ₹7,000 a year plus GST. Zoho Books, which is accounting software with accounts payable (AP) features, costs ₹749 to ₹7,999 a month per organisation on annual billing. AI Accountant, Clear, Volopay and Open publish no price list and quote on a demo.

How do Indian vendors price accounts payable automation?

Four pricing models cover almost every quote you will see.

  • Per page. You buy a pack of pages and use it up as you scan bills. Docs by Ira works this way: ₹200 for 200 pages or ₹3,000 for 3,000 pages, each valid for one year, with 18% GST added. A five-page bill uses five pages.
  • Flat yearly fee. One price covers unlimited entries. Vyapar TaxOne lists its Unlimited Plan at ₹7,000 a year against a list price of ₹14,000, with GST extra.
  • Per organisation or per user, by month. Cloud accounting software prices by plan. Zoho Books runs from ₹749 to ₹7,999 a month per organisation on annual billing. RealBooks sells in bundles of five users.
  • Custom quote. The vendor prices by your bill volume, entities, integrations and set-up. AI Accountant, Clear, Volopay and Open all work this way.

What pushes the price up or down?

  • Invoice volume and pages. On a page pack, cost rises with every page. On a flat plan, volume costs nothing extra.
  • Users and companies. Zoho Books includes 3 to 25 users depending on the plan, and an extra user costs ₹150 a month on annual billing. Hisabkitab charges ₹500 a year for an extra user and ₹1,000 a year for an extra company.
  • Tally integration. A tool inside or on top of Tally avoids a migration. Docs by Ira needs TallyPrime 7.1 and an active TSS, so add the TSS renewal to its cost if yours has lapsed. A tool with its own ledger means moving your books.
  • GST and TDS handling. Reading the GSTIN, HSN code and tax split from each bill is the baseline. Vendor master checks, TDS and reverse charge tagging, and GSTR-2B reconciliation are where tools differ.
  • Bill quality. Clean digital PDFs are the cheap case. Docs by Ira states that handwritten documents are not supported, so a business with many handwritten bills needs a tool that reads them.
  • Approvals and payments. Approval rules and vendor payouts move you towards the tools that quote on request.
  • Implementation. The published plans above list no separate set-up fee. On a custom quote, ask what is charged once and what is charged every year.

What does manual invoice processing cost an Indian team?

Tally Solutions estimates that a manually processed invoice costs an Indian business ₹60 to ₹150 on a fully loaded basis, once salary, overhead, error correction and approval delays are counted. The direct data entry is only about ₹13 of that, based on four minutes per invoice. Its estimate rises with volume: ₹60 per invoice at 50 invoices a month, ₹80 at 100, ₹100 at 200, and ₹150 at 500. The same article puts an automated invoice at ₹12 to ₹30. Tally sells an automation product, so treat these as a vendor's estimate.

Our own case studies give a sense of the time involved:

  • Sinnova Tech Engineering handles 120 purchase bills a month in under 1 hour. Sinnova estimates extraction and prediction accuracy at about 95%.
  • Laundry Labs takes 1 to 1.5 minutes per bill from PDF to a reviewed Tally entry, with 98% of entries prepared correctly.
  • Vigor Solar Energy reports 60 to 80% less time per entry.
  • In a demo estimate for a three-city retailer typing 9,000 bills a month, moving from 10 minutes of entry to 2 minutes of review per bill came to more than 1,200 hours a month. The retailer put the cost of one AP employee at ₹20,000 a month. This is an estimate from the demo's inputs, not a measured result.

To check payback, multiply the minutes your team spends per bill by your monthly bill count and by what an hour of that time costs, then compare the result with the all-in yearly price of the tool.

Where AI Accountant fits

AI Accountant is a Tally-native accounting automation platform for Indian businesses and CA firms. For accounts payable, it reads vendor bills as PDFs, images, scans, handwritten bills or Excel files, and extracts the vendor, GSTIN, invoice number, dates, line items, HSN codes and taxes. It flags vendor mismatches and duplicates, sends every bill for review, and syncs approved entries to Tally. The details are on the vendor bill matching page.

It is built for businesses with in-house Tally teams. It does not replace Tally, it does not make vendor payments, and it does not run approval workflows.

What should you have ready before you ask for a quote?

  • Purchase bills per month, averaged over the last 12 months.
  • Pages per bill, and the share that is scanned or handwritten.
  • Tally companies and GSTINs to be covered.
  • Your TallyPrime release and whether TSS is active.
  • Who reviews bills before they post to Tally.
  • Whether the tool must also pay vendors.
  • The all-in yearly price, with GST shown separately.

FAQ

How much does accounts payable automation cost in India?

Published prices for tools that automate purchase-bill entry start at ₹200 for a 200-page Docs by Ira pack and ₹7,000 a year for Vyapar TaxOne (formerly Suvit). Cloud accounting software with accounts payable features, such as Zoho Books, runs from ₹749 to ₹7,999 a month per organisation on annual billing. Clear, Volopay and Open quote on request.

What does accounts payable automation cost per invoice?

Few Indian tools charge per invoice. Docs by Ira charges by the page, at ₹200 for 200 pages or ₹3,000 for 3,000 pages, so a one-page bill costs about ₹1 before GST and your own review time. Tally Solutions estimates the all-in cost of an automated invoice at ₹12 to ₹30, against ₹60 to ₹150 for a manual one.

Is GST charged on top of the published price?

Usually, yes. Tally shows 18% GST as a separate line on Docs by Ira packs and TSS renewals, and Vyapar TaxOne says GST is charged in addition to its listed price. Zoho Books and RealBooks say their prices exclude local taxes. Ask for the all-in figure in writing.

Does the price include implementation?

The published prices in this guide list no separate set-up fee. Tools that connect to banks or larger ERPs price integration into a custom quote, so ask what is charged once and what is charged every year.

What does AI Accountant cost?

AI Accountant does not publish a price list. Pricing scales with your transaction volume and is quoted on a demo.

Does accounts payable automation work with Tally?

Yes, several tools do. Docs by Ira runs inside TallyPrime 7.1 or later with an active TSS. Vyapar TaxOne, VouchrIt and AI Accountant work on top of Tally and post entries to it. Zoho Books and RealBooks are accounting systems of their own, so using them means keeping your books there.
