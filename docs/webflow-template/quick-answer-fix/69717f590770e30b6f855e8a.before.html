How AI accounting software is charged in India

Every price in the table follows one of four patterns. The pattern tells you how the bill will grow.

  • Licence plus yearly renewal. TallyPrime sells a lifetime licence (₹22,500 for Silver, ₹67,500 for Gold) and then charges a yearly TSS renewal (₹4,500 or ₹13,500). Docs by Ira gives 100 free pages with TSS and sells more pages separately. You pay most in year one.
  • Monthly plan per organisation. Zoho Books charges per organisation a month, with five paid plans from ₹749 to ₹7,999 when billed yearly. Each plan caps users, and extra users cost ₹150 a month each.
  • Flat yearly plan. Vyapar, Vyapar TaxOne, hisabkitab and Flick AI charge one amount a year. hisabkitab limits companies and users, and Flick AI limits invoices parsed each month.
  • Quote by volume. AI Accountant and Qosh AI give a price after they understand your volumes. Our product page puts it this way: "Pricing is volume-based. We share a custom quote during the demo after understanding your bill and bank statement volumes."

With a quote you pay for the documents you process, not for seats you may not use. Bring a month of real bills and bank statements to the demo.

What moves the price of AI accounting software

  • GST. Almost every list price excludes it. TallyPrime and Flick AI show "+18% GST", hisabkitab and Vyapar TaxOne say GST is charged in addition, and Zoho Books says local taxes are extra.
  • Users and companies. Zoho Books caps users by plan. hisabkitab caps both companies and users. TallyPrime Gold, for several users, costs three times Silver.
  • Document volume. Flick AI Pro costs more for five times the invoices. AI Accountant quotes on your bill and bank statement volume.
  • Billing term. Zoho Books Standard is ₹899 paid monthly and ₹749 billed yearly.
  • Discounts that may end. Vyapar TaxOne's ₹7,000 and Vyapar's prices are shown against higher list prices. Ask what the renewal will cost.

AI accounting software for businesses vs managed service

When to choose standalone AI accounting software for businesses

  • Scope is simple with basic profit and loss and straightforward GST.
  • Strong internal finance skills are available.
  • You want automation and visibility. Advisory is not required.
  • Compliance is minimal.
  • Cost sensitivity drives the decision.

When to choose or add a managed service

  • Compliance is complex across many entities: GST, TDS, income tax, and ROC filings.
  • Your team lacks bandwidth or specific expertise.
  • You want end to end execution by a CA with software like visibility.
  • You value continuous compliance oversight and proactive advice.
  • You want to free internal teams to focus on strategy.

Hybrid approach

Many teams run a hybrid. AI software for speed and visibility. CA led managed service for execution and assurance. You get automation plus judgment, the best of both worlds.

Practical tip: start with AI for the data layer and dashboards. Add a CA managed service for filings, reconciliations, and control reviews as complexity grows.

Where a CA led managed service fits with AI accounting software for businesses

Virtual Accounting by AI Accountant blends professional execution with system driven visibility. You see the work in a live dashboard while a CA team handles accounting and compliance end to end.

What to check before you pay for AI accounting software

  1. Does it work on your Tally books, or does it need you to move them?
  2. Is GST included in the price you were shown?
  3. How many users, companies and documents does the plan allow?
  4. Which plan holds the AI features you want?
  5. Does a person review each entry before it posts?
  6. What will the renewal cost once the discount ends?
  7. Can you test it on your own bills and bank statements first?

Evaluation checklist to choose AI accounting software for businesses

Compliance capabilities

  • GST support for GSTR 1, GSTR 3B, and GSTR 9 and GSTR 9C.
  • E invoice enablement with IRN integration.
  • TDS forms support for 26Q, 24Q, and 27Q plus challan tracking.
  • Income tax ITR preparation and advance tax reminders.

Data security and privacy

  • Encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Role based access controls and audit logs.
  • Data localization where required.
  • Certifications such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2.

Integration breadth and reliability

  • Bank and payment gateway connections with stable sync.
  • Invoicing tool connectors such as Shopify, plus custom APIs.
  • Payroll and ERP compatibility, including Tally.
  • Clear testing processes and service level commitments.

AI accuracy and explainability

  • Published accuracy rates for categorization and reconciliations.
  • Easy review and correction of AI choices.
  • Transparent reasons for anomaly and fraud flags.
  • Tuning based on your chart of accounts and business model.

Support and onboarding

  • Implementation and data migration assistance.
  • Training for your finance team.
  • Ongoing support by chat, email, and phone.
  • Regular product updates on a clear schedule.

Pricing and scalability

  • Subscription model and total cost of ownership.
  • Tiers based on users and transaction volume.
  • Scaling limits as you grow.

Vendor credibility

  • Transparent roadmap that aligns with your needs.
  • Track record and customer references.
  • Strong SLAs and uptime guarantees.

FAQ

What is the price of AI accounting software in India?

Published prices run from about ₹2,999 a year for hisabkitab Launch to ₹7,999 a month for Zoho Books Ultimate, before GST. TallyPrime costs ₹750 a month or ₹22,500 for a lifetime Silver licence, Vyapar TaxOne (formerly Suvit) ₹7,000 a year and Flick AI ₹4,999 or ₹11,999 a year. AI Accountant and Qosh AI, which work as a layer on Tally, quote on request.

How much does AI Accountant cost?

AI Accountant does not publish a price list. Pricing is volume-based, and a custom quote is shared during the demo once your bill and bank statement volumes are understood. The quote scales with the size of the company.

Which AI accounting software is free in India?

Zoho Books has a free plan for businesses with revenue up to ₹25 lakh a year, and Refrens lets you create 15 documents free. Flick AI offers a 14-day free trial, Vyapar TaxOne a 7-day free trial and Qosh AI a free trial with no card.

Is GST included in the listed prices?

Usually not. TallyPrime and Flick AI add 18% GST to the listed price, hisabkitab and Vyapar TaxOne state that GST is charged in addition, and Zoho Books lists prices exclusive of local taxes. Add it before you compare totals.

Do I need to buy new accounting software to use AI with Tally?

No. Docs by Ira works inside TallyPrime, and layers such as AI Accountant, Vyapar TaxOne and Qosh AI read your documents and send entries to the Tally company you already have. Zoho Books, hisabkitab, Flick AI and Vyapar are separate ledgers.

Are Booke AI and Dext priced in rupees?

Their published prices are in US dollars for overseas buyers. Booke AI works inside QuickBooks Online or Xero, and Dext sends captured documents to other accounting software. Neither lists Tally.

How should a founder evaluate whether to start with AI accounting software or add a CA managed service immediately

Start with AI software if your entity structure is simple, internal finance can handle monthly routines, and you mainly need automation and live visibility. Add a CA managed service from day one if you have multi entity operations, frequent GST reconciliations, TDS obligations across payroll and vendors, and limited internal bandwidth. The hybrid approach (software plus managed service) is increasingly common because it avoids over reliance on automation alone.
