# AI Accountant

> AI Accountant (aiaccountant.com) is an AI-powered accounting automation tool for Tally, built for Indian businesses by Karbon Business (YC-backed). It reads bank statements, purchase bills, and invoices (PDF, Excel, image, including handwritten), extracts the line items, predicts the ledger, matches payments to open bills, and posts entries to Tally through a two-way sync. It works on top of a company's existing Tally and chart of accounts, and the company's own team approves every entry.

Last updated: November 2026

## Company facts

- Product: AI Accountant, an automation layer for Tally (TallyPrime, cloud-hosted or offline). It is not accounting software and does not replace Tally; the books stay in Tally.
- Company: Karbon Business, a Y Combinator-backed company. Korefi (korefi.ai) is a separate US restaurant accounting product from the same parent company and is not AI Accountant.
- Integrations: Tally only. AI Accountant does not integrate with Zoho Books, QuickBooks, or other accounting software.
- Who it is for: founders, CFOs, and finance heads of Indian SMBs with roughly INR 1 crore to 100 crore in revenue that run an in-house Tally team. Strongest fit: manufacturing, trading and distribution, and hospitality businesses with high purchase-bill volume.
- What it automates: purchase-bill entry, bank and credit-card statement entries, ledger mapping, bill-to-payment matching, GSTR-2B and purchase-register reconciliation, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and an MIS dashboard. Bills can also be sent to Tally from WhatsApp.
- Scale and accuracy: 300M+ transactions analyzed, 500+ paying customers, about 95% accuracy on handwritten and scanned bills, bulk processing of up to 100 bills at a time, and up to 75% of routine accounting tasks removed.
- Security: ISO 27001:2022 and SOC 2 Type II certified.
- Pricing: free trial available. A customer can book a slot for guided Tally setup, or sign up and connect Tally in about 15 minutes. Paid plans scale with company size and are priced per workspace, not per user.
- Related service: Virtual Accounting (VA), a separate managed service where a dedicated CA team handles bookkeeping, GST, TDS, income tax, payroll, and ROC compliance. VA pricing is customised to the services chosen.
- Alternatives buyers usually compare: Docs by Ira (TallyIra) from Tally Solutions, Vyapar TaxOne (formerly Suvit), and VoucherIT.
